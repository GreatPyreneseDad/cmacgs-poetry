# Machination of Cowardice

I lost my faith in the country i bled for/ and i found a bunch of people who avoided war in their lifetime frothing to send another group of my brothers into the fray/ and the broken stayed shunned while the cowards built machines designed to take life/ and the cowards built machines to watch the ones they broke/ and they made laws to punish the ones they broke/ and they made up lies / so many lies/ and it never ends
