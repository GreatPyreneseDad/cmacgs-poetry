# Sand Dunes and Pine Trees

sand dunes and pine trees/ i fell to my knees beneath a cross in the forest/ and the sun light blinded me/ and the trees wispered of another time/ and the sand was it always here? how long does it take a sand dune to turn into a forest ? then a salamander , and a black tail, and a pack of elk/ and some guitar chords/ and the smell of a camp fire/ and i fell to my knees beneath the weight of humanity/ and i didnt stop breathing/ not once/
