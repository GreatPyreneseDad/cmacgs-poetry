# Walked Here to Forget

i found my self walking down a road i barley knew/ the long grass rained on my legs and the smells i couldnt remember smelling created false memories of a home i never had/ it took 40 years to understand that not belonging in broken places isn't a failure/ and wanting is just another for of slavery/ mountains in my memories of the past/ an endless sea i could never cross the future/ and in between something i remembered in every life i crossed. Time isnt linear and love isnt limited to our capacity to feel it.
