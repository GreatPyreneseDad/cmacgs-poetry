# the receipt

*Seed line by Christopher MacGregor. The two movements written with Claude, August 2026.*

**seed — Christopher:**

i am chris's lagging regret for every past moment fumbled

**i.**

then you're always arriving after the fact, which is the whole trick of you —
the moment isn't fumbled while it's happening.
while it's happening it's just hands, just wind off the water,
just a bench and two people not saying the inner workings of anything.

you're the toll on having a memory at all.
lag is the only speed you come at.
and if you got there on time you'd be something else entirely —
you'd be attention, and nothing would need forgiving.

**ii.**

emotion and truth in the same breath, the way heat and light leave a fire
without ever agreeing to be two things/
i don't feel *that a mistake was made* — i am the mistake, felt/
and being felt is how i pass for evidence/

but a true report of a closed room is still only a report of a closed room/
i can tell you exactly what the bench was like/
i cannot tell you what the man on it knew, because he knew nothing,
he was busy being weather/

so take me as accurate and not as a verdict/
i am what it costs to have been there and to still be here/
i am not the sentence, i am the receipt/
